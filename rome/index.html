罗马史A History of Rome

2017 年起在公众号「烟霞Nebula」上连载A serial on the WeChat account Yanxia Nebula, begun in 2017

2017 年夏天开了这个公众号，想把读到的罗马故事一篇篇写下来：从特洛伊陷落后的神话写起，经过七位国王，一直写到共和国初年的辛辛纳图斯。后来毕业、工作，连载停在了第三十八篇。几年后这个公众号成了烟霞的阵地，罗马也时不时回到烟霞的分享里。

文章都在公众号上，点标题可以读原文。希望以后能慢慢把它写完。

I started the account in the summer of 2017 to write down the Roman stories I was reading, one post at a time: from the legends after the fall of Troy, through the seven kings, to Cincinnatus in the early Republic. Then came graduation and work, and the serial stopped at part thirty-eight. Years later the account became the home of Yanxia, and Rome keeps coming back in its talks.

The posts are on WeChat and in Chinese; each title links to the original. I hope to finish it, slowly.
